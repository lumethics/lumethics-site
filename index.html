Proven Results

Real outcomes delivered across regulated, high-growth, and complex environments — measurable impact at enterprise scale.

Challenge

Ageing infrastructure unable to support rapid growth, clinical uptime at risk, and rising IT costs.

Action

Full infrastructure transformation — hybrid Azure IaaS/PaaS, SD-WAN/SASE via Zscaler, ServiceNow ITSM, Microsoft 365 and AI Copilot across 800+ users. ISO 27001 and GDPR governance embedded.

Result

99.9% uptime and 98% SLA compliance maintained. 30% licensing cost reduction. 22% operational savings. Infrastructure scaled seamlessly to support an £80M business.

Challenge

Rapid EMEA/LATAM expansion with fragmented IT, rising telecoms costs, and no standardised operations.

Action

Enterprise IT strategy across EMEA/LATAM. Consolidated infrastructure and data centres, virtualisation and DevOps automation. Vendor renegotiation. Cloud-first strategy, 24×7 help desk, GDPR/ISO 27001 compliance frameworks.

Result

Multi-million-pound cost savings (71% telecoms reduction, 40%+ infrastructure optimisation). Security and compliance frameworks that enabled £23.5M in sales. 35% fewer support tickets, 80%+ first-contact resolution, and 99.95% uptime across 5+ countries.

Challenge

Post-merger integration requiring digital transformation with no standardised IT governance.

Action

EMEA transformation programme leading 52 IT staff across multiple countries. Enterprise ERP implementation, IT infrastructure modernisation, PRINCE2 governance, ITIL service management, ISO 9001/20000 compliance across all sites.

Result

Successful merger integration on time. Minimal operational disruption. Full compliance achieved across all regulated environments.

Challenge

A fragmented server estate spread across 22 sites in a highly regulated financial environment, driving up operational cost, risk, and complexity.

Action

Consolidated 900 servers across 22 sites into a centralised data centre. Standardised server, messaging, and network infrastructure with regulated-grade controls and 24×7 support.

Result

A single, resilient data centre supporting 20,000+ users. Reduced operational complexity and cost, with a strengthened compliance posture across the regulated estate.
